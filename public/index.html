How to link

  1. 1

    Open WhatsApp on your phone

    Make sure you're on the account you want to link

  2. 2

    Go to Settings → Linked Devices

    Android: ⋮ menu · iOS: bottom tabs

  3. 3

    Tap "Link a Device"

    Approve with your phone PIN if asked

  4. 4

    Point at the QR code

    Auto-refreshes every ~20 seconds

Tip: Keep this tab open during linking. If it closes, the QR will still refresh — just come back and rescan.
